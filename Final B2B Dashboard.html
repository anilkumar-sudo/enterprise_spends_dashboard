Forecast Method
The forecast uses the same dashboard spend records already loaded into the app. It blends two signals: current run-rate across the active fiscal year and the historical month-to-month seasonality visible in the available spend pattern. Locked months stay actual, while the current and future months are distributed using normalized seasonal weights.
